## How to work this list

1. Read the Open table. Blocking bugs first, then bugs, then everything else.
2. For each one you act on, write a **Triage:** line under its entry — what you decided
   and why. Free text; it is preserved across pulls.
3. Close it in the app (Help → the report → Close) when it ships. The next pull moves it
   to Closed, with its triage line intact.
4. The seat is load-bearing: a report from the owner seat is about what the owner can
   see, not what the builder can.
